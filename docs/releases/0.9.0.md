## New photos during the initial scan

Reopen PhotoManiak during an unfinished scan, or tap **Play** after pausing, to check selected kDrive folders for new files before continuing capture-date analysis. New photos appear in the timeline after folder discovery, with provisional dates while their metadata is pending. The scan reuses capture dates already verified for unchanged files.

A manual pause survives app restarts. Tap **Play** to continue.

## Downloads

- **FOSS:** gallery without the proprietary Google Cast SDK.
- **nonFOSS:** gallery with Google Cast.

Both editions support Android 8+ and use the existing distribution certificate for in-place updates.

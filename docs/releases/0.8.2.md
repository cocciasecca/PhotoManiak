## Resumable scanning

- During the initial scan, the toolbar shows **Pause / Play** instead of Refresh. A manual pause persists across app restarts.
- **Play** checks selected folders for new files, then continues reading missing capture dates without rereading completed metadata for unchanged files.
- Scanning pauses when the app goes into the background and resumes when you return, unless manually paused.
- Progress is saved after every listing page and metadata result, including across app restarts. The previous timeline stays available until folder discovery completes.
- Temporary network errors are retried with backoff. Invalid credentials and permission errors remain visible.
- After the initial scan finishes, incremental refresh becomes available and runs on app startup. Full refresh is available in Settings.

- **nonFOSS:** the same gallery plus Google Cast; casting requires Google Play services.

## Update download preference

PhotoManiak asks whether to download updates automatically the first time it starts without a saved choice. This includes in-place upgrades from builds that did not offer the choice. If you already chose automatic downloads or in-app notices only, the app remembers that choice.

Automatic downloads open the Android installer for the installed edition. Android asks you to confirm installation. Change your preference in **Settings → Updates**.

## Downloads

- **FOSS:** gallery without the proprietary Google Cast SDK.
- **nonFOSS:** gallery with Google Cast.

Both editions require Android 8+ and use the existing distribution certificate for in-place updates.
